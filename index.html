Scoring model

Peer adoption

Share of other accounts in the same industry that already own the product. If most hospitals bought it, the next hospital probably needs it too.

Product affinity

Lift from co-ownership across the whole base: P(product | owns X) ÷ P(product). Uses the strongest signal among products the account already owns.

Size & fit

Log-scaled employee count, so bigger organizations score higher without drowning out everyone else.

Engagement

Engagement score decayed by days since the last touch. A warm account converts faster than a cold one.

CSV columns: account, industry, region, employees, engagement, last_touch_days plus one 0/1 column per product (data_platform, ai_assistants, automation, security, hybrid_cloud, observability). Deal sizes use illustrative list prices scaled by company size.
